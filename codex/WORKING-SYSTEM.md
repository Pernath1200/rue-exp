## How it is used

**Sniper in.** Lesson → capture (marking sheets and handwritten spoken notes now; **Plaud** later) → error report (main errors, the rule, a link) → they open that unit, it explains the issue, they do the exercises, they enjoy it, they go **aha**, the bug is dead.

**They do not stop there.** The unit has to be good enough that they want the **next unit linked to it**, then fill gaps **below and around** it, and they want to **see their tree growing**. Search (look up a grammar or vocab issue, get the rule and the practice) is the other door — **not this phase**.

If the same mistake comes back: they weren’t paying attention, they didn’t use the app, or they used it and **the teaching wasn’t good enough to stick**. A per-student error log is later.
